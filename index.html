01 — About

From toy cars to the camera.

It started when AR's parents gave him toy cars and trucks. That turned into an obsession with supercars, then hours of video games, then watching automotive videos — and finally, making his own.

From spotting cars around NCR to travelling down South, he keeps putting himself in new environments. The dream: become a professional automotive videographer and travel the world capturing different places and different car cultures.
